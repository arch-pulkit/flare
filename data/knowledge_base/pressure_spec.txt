SKYROOT-M1 Propulsion Subsystem — Tank Pressure Specification
Document ID: doc_003 | Tier: SUPPORTING | Subsystem: PROP

The propellant tank nominal operating pressure range for SKYROOT-M1 is 200000 Pa to 350000 Pa (2.0 bar to 3.5 bar absolute). The regulated pressure at the thruster inlet is maintained at 210000 Pa to 240000 Pa by the pressure regulator assembly. Tank pressure below 200000 Pa indicates propellant depletion or regulator failure and requires mission operations team assessment before continuing propulsion operations. Tank pressure above 350000 Pa indicates regulator failure in the open position and represents a structural safety concern.

The propellant tank maximum design pressure is 500000 Pa. The proof pressure test was conducted at 750000 Pa. The burst pressure limit is 1000000 Pa. The operating pressure range of 200000 to 350000 Pa provides a safety factor of 1.4 against the maximum design pressure. Under no operating condition should the tank pressure exceed 400000 Pa, which is the upper safety limit established by the propulsion system safety analysis. Sustained pressure above 350000 Pa requires immediate valve closure and investigation.

The blowdown ratio from beginning of life to end of life spans from 350000 Pa at full propellant load to 200000 Pa when the propellant is nominally depleted. Tank pressure below 200000 Pa with propellant mass remaining indicates a pressure regulator leak or propellant line blockage. Verify propellant utilization efficiency is consistent with the nominal blowdown curve before concluding a propellant anomaly. Monitor the pressure decay rate during non-firing periods to detect micro-leaks in propellant fittings and valve seats.

The fill-and-drain valve assembly must maintain leak-tight sealing at all tank pressures from 0 to 350000 Pa. Any detected pressure drop rate exceeding 500 Pa per hour during ground hold or on-orbit non-firing periods requires investigation. The propellant isolation valve is the primary safing device and must be verified closed during all non-operational periods. Pressure readings from the primary and redundant pressure transducers must agree within 2000 Pa; discrepancy beyond this tolerance indicates sensor degradation.

The tank pressure telemetry channel TANK_PRESSURE provides continuous pressure monitoring at 1 Hz sampling rate. The warning threshold for low tank pressure is 200000 Pa and for high tank pressure is 350000 Pa. Investigate any reading outside the nominal range of 200000 to 350000 Pa. Reduce propellant flow and verify regulator performance if tank pressure exceeds the upper warning threshold of 350000 Pa during thruster operations.
