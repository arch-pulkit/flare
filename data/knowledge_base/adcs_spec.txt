SKYROOT-M1 Attitude Determination and Control System — Reaction Wheel Specification
Document ID: doc_004 | Tier: CRITICAL | Subsystem: ADCS

The SKYROOT-M1 attitude control system uses a four-reaction-wheel assembly for three-axis momentum management. The nominal operating speed range for each reaction wheel is 1000 rpm to 6000 rpm. Operation within this speed range provides the torque authority required for nominal attitude control, orbital maneuver support, and momentum unloading via magnetic torquer rods. Reaction wheel speed below 1000 rpm results in insufficient control authority and may cause attitude pointing errors exceeding mission requirements. Speed above 6000 rpm approaches the mechanical bearing limit and risks premature bearing wear.

The reaction wheel assembly REACTION_WHEEL_RPM telemetry channel reports wheel speed in revolutions per minute at 2 Hz sampling rate. The nominal control law maintains wheel speed within the range of 1500 rpm to 5500 rpm during steady-state attitude hold operations, reserving 500 rpm margin at each end of the operating range for transient maneuver authority. Wheel speed sustained above 5500 rpm indicates excessive external torque disturbances and requires momentum unloading via the magnetic torquer rods before speed reaches the upper limit of 6000 rpm.

The minimum wheel speed threshold of 1000 rpm is the lower bound for reliable speed measurement by the wheel tachometer. Speed control below 1000 rpm is undefined in the attitude control law and may result in oscillatory behavior. If any wheel drops below 1000 rpm during nominal attitude control, the ADCS controller shall initiate an automatic momentum unloading sequence and log a WHEEL_UNDERSPEED event. Investigate any sustained wheel speed below 1000 rpm to identify the root cause before resuming normal attitude operations.

The reaction wheel bearing lifetime is rated for 5 years at continuous operation within the nominal speed range of 1000 to 6000 rpm. Operating consistently above 5000 rpm accelerates bearing degradation and reduces mission lifetime. The wheel motor current draw is nominally 120 mA at 3500 rpm and increases to 350 mA at 6000 rpm. Elevated motor current at a given speed indicates bearing friction increase and is an early indicator of bearing failure. Monitor wheel motor current telemetry as a secondary health indicator alongside wheel speed telemetry.

Wheel desaturation maneuvers use the magnetic torquer rods to exchange momentum with the Earth magnetic field, reducing wheel speed from near-saturation levels above 5500 rpm back toward the nominal operating center of 3500 rpm. Desaturation frequency depends on the residual magnetic dipole and atmospheric drag torques, typically occurring every 2 to 6 orbits. A desaturation rate higher than one per orbit indicates excessive external torque disturbances and requires attitude investigation. Verify reaction wheel speed is within the nominal range of 1000 to 6000 rpm after each desaturation maneuver.

The safing speed limit for reaction wheels is 6200 rpm, above which the wheel controller applies maximum braking torque. The absolute mechanical speed limit is 6500 rpm; exceedance of this speed will trigger an autonomous safe-mode transition and wheel power-off to protect the bearing assembly. Operate the reaction wheel assembly within the nominal speed range of 1000 to 6000 rpm to ensure attitude control performance and bearing longevity throughout the mission design lifetime.
